About Me

I'm an Australian mixed media artist — essentially self-taught, with the help of YouTube, Patreon, and online classes from a few talented artists working in different mediums. Of course, the hours of trial and error have been my greatest teacher. I live in Newcastle, NSW, with my family and our dogs. Art is a huge and colourful part of my life; I can spend countless hours working on a single piece, completely immersed in my happy place.

I'm a maximalist — in my home, my art, and how I express myself. I'll never be an artist who specialises in just one subject or medium. I work in soft pastels, gouache, watercolour, and my true love: coloured pencils. Sometimes I use them individually, and other times I combine them all — whatever helps bring a piece to life. I always use artist-quality materials and strive for lightfastness and longevity in my work.

I'm always inspired by the macro, close-up view of life — animals and flowers in particular. One of my favourite things to do is create portraits of loved pets, and I've also created an artwork of a friend's wedding bouquet. Seeing that my work brings joy and everlasting memories to someone is incredibly rewarding.
